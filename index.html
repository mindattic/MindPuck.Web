MindPuck

The puck goes where you look. Bring it to rest on the amber pad.

  • Magnetic (the default): the puck is pulled to the point you look at. Lead it round the obstacles.
  • Repulsor: the puck is pushed away from where you look. Look where you don't want it to go.
  • Press F1 at any time to switch between the two.
